Hi, there! This is the repository for the files of the **Footprints on Campus (FOC)** Group, published under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 International license.

We hold an unofficial group of members in our school, but have no association with the school or others. We record today's scenery of the campus so that future people can look back and appreciate it.
